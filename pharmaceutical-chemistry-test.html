Question 1 of 20

Which test is commonly used to identify chloride ions?

Explanation: Chloride ions form a white precipitate of silver chloride with AgNO₃.
Question 2 of 20

What is the main functional group in carboxylic acids?

Explanation: Carboxylic acids contain the –COOH (carboxyl) group.
Question 3 of 20

Aspirin is chemically known as:

Explanation: Aspirin is acetylsalicylic acid, formed by acetylation of salicylic acid.
Question 4 of 20

The chemical name of paracetamol is:

Explanation: Paracetamol is N-(4-hydroxyphenyl)acetamide, also called acetaminophen.
Question 5 of 20

Which reagent gives a violet colour with phenolic compounds?

Explanation: Ferric chloride commonly gives coloured complexes with phenolic compounds.
Question 6 of 20

The molecular formula of sodium bicarbonate is:

Explanation: Sodium bicarbonate is NaHCO₃.
Question 7 of 20

Which of the following is an antacid?

Explanation: Magnesium hydroxide neutralizes gastric acid and is used as an antacid.
Question 8 of 20

The oxidation state of sulfur in H₂SO₄ is:

Explanation: Hydrogen is +1 and oxygen is −2; sulfur therefore has an oxidation state of +6.
Question 9 of 20

Which compound is used as a reducing agent in volumetric analysis?

Explanation: Sodium thiosulfate is widely used as a reducing titrant, especially in iodometry.
Question 10 of 20

Limit test for chloride is based on formation of:

Explanation: Chloride reacts with silver nitrate to produce AgCl turbidity.
Question 11 of 20

Limit test for sulfate uses:

Explanation: Sulfate produces barium sulfate turbidity with barium chloride.
Question 12 of 20

Which indicator is commonly used in acid-base titration?

Explanation: Phenolphthalein is a common acid-base indicator.
Question 13 of 20

The pH of a neutral aqueous solution at 25°C is approximately:

Explanation: At 25°C, neutral water has a pH of approximately 7.
Question 14 of 20

Which functional group is characteristic of aldehydes?

Explanation: Aldehydes contain the terminal –CHO group.
Question 15 of 20

Which drug contains a sulfonamide functional group?

Explanation: Sulfamethoxazole is a sulfonamide antibacterial drug.
Question 16 of 20

What is the purpose of a buffer solution?

Explanation: Buffers resist changes in pH when small amounts of acid or base are added.
Question 17 of 20

Which reaction is characteristic of primary aromatic amines?

Explanation: Primary aromatic amines can undergo diazotization with nitrous acid at low temperature.
Question 18 of 20

Which of the following is an inorganic pharmaceutical?

Explanation: Ferrous sulfate is an inorganic medicinal compound used as an iron supplement.
Question 19 of 20

What does pKa indicate?

Explanation: pKa is related to acid strength; lower pKa generally indicates a stronger acid.
Question 20 of 20

Which analytical technique is based on absorption of ultraviolet/visible radiation?

Explanation: UV-Visible spectrophotometry measures absorption of UV or visible radiation by substances.
